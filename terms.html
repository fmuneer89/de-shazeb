Orders & payment

This website currently offers catalogue browsing and a preview bag. Checkout is a demonstration: it does not send an order, reserve stock or collect payment. An item in your bag is not a confirmed purchase. Please do not enter real personal or payment details into the preview checkout.

Before any purchase, obtain confirmation from De-Shahzaib of the item, collar size, stock, total price, delivery charge and available payment method. Cash on Delivery and online card payments are not offered through this website at present.

Products & prices

Prices are displayed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). Screen settings and lighting can affect how colours appear. Check the product photographs and description carefully. Available size choices are catalogue options; they do not guarantee stock. Any price or availability correction should be confirmed before payment.

Delivery

Dispatch is from Karachi, Pakistan. Delivery availability, shipping charges and estimated arrival time must be confirmed for your address before payment. No free-delivery threshold or fixed delivery time is currently guaranteed on this website.

7-day returns & exchanges

Request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. Shirts must be unworn, unwashed and in like-new condition, with their tags and original packaging. Keep your proof of purchase and contact De-Shahzaib through the same verified channel used to arrange your order before sending anything back.

Include your order reference, the reason for the request and photographs if an item is faulty, damaged or incorrect. Return instructions, shipping costs, the refund method and expected processing time will be confirmed when the request is reviewed. Exchange availability depends on stock. This policy does not exclude applicable consumer rights.

Choose your collar size

Sizes are expressed in inches. Measure loosely around the base of your neck with a soft tape, leaving room for one finger. Compare with a comfortable shirt you already own. Collar size alone does not establish chest, shoulder, sleeve or overall fit; ask for the finished shirt measurements before buying if you are unsure.

Shirt care

Follow the care label on your individual shirt. Do not assume every fabric has the same washing or ironing requirements.

Privacy & browser storage

The bag, saved shirts and motion preference use storage in your browser. They are not an account and do not synchronise between devices. You can remove them by clearing this website’s stored data in your browser settings.

The current preview checkout does not submit its form details to an order service. Website hosting providers may process technical request information under their own policies. If you arrange a purchase separately, ask how your contact and delivery information will be used before sharing it. Never submit card numbers, passwords or banking credentials through this preview.

Customer care

A verified business contact has not yet been published here. Until it is added, use the verified De-Shahzaib channel through which you originally contacted the business. Do not send payment to an unverified contact.
