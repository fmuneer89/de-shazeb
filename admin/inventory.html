First-time setup

In Cloudflare, open Workers & Pages → de-shazeb → Settings → Variables and Secrets. Add an encrypted secret named INVENTORY_ADMIN_PASSWORD with a unique password of at least 16 characters, then redeploy. Keep the password private. There is no default password.
