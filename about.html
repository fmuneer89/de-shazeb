Crafted For Confidence

De-Shahzaib is a Karachi-based menswear label making premium formal shirts for men who want a fit built around them, not a size chart.

Our Story

De-Shahzaib was founded on a simple belief — that formalwear should feel as composed as the men who wear it. Every shirt is designed with quiet precision, cut for structure and finished for comfort.

This is our founding collection, and every piece carries that first-run attention to detail.

Our Fabrics

We work with cotton poplin, brushed oxford, and twill — chosen for how they hold shape through a full working day, not just how they photograph.

Fabric sourcing and finishing details for each shirt are listed on its product page.
