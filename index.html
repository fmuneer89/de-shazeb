Delivery across Pakistan +

Dispatch is from Karachi. Delivery within Pakistan is free on orders over PKR 4,000 after discounts. Delivery charges for smaller orders and international destinations, and estimated timing, are confirmed before payment. Delivery information ↗

Returns & exchanges +

Request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. Shirts must be unworn, unwashed and in like-new condition, with tags and original packaging. Read the return policy ↗

How do I choose my size? +

Shirts use collar sizes in inches. See the size guide on each product page.

Payment options +

Place your request on WhatsApp. We will confirm stock, delivery charges and available payment arrangements before accepting your order. No payment is collected on this website.
