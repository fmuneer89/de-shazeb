Delivery across Pakistan +

Orders dispatch from Karachi within 2–4 business days. Delivery typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your city. Free shipping applies above PKR 5,000; the fee below that amount needs to be configured.

Returns & exchanges +

The current website allows requests within 14 days of delivery for unworn shirts in their original condition. Confirm the return process before launch.

How do I choose my size? +

Measure around the base of your neck. Shirts use collar sizes in inches.

Payment options +

About this template

A separate dark design for De-Shahzaib. Your live website has not been changed.

  • Existing logo, campaign and shirt photographs are reused without changing the fabric or product design.
  • Six reference products and prices were collected on 12 October 2026. Prices are not a live feed.
  • Descriptions, category grouping and collar availability are illustrative. No reviews or stock claims are invented.
  • The bag and saved items stay in this browser. Checkout is a demonstration; personal details are not saved or sent.
  • Template tools edit a local browser copy. Download the updated index.html and replace index.html in your GitHub project to publish catalog edits.
  • Real orders, admin login, inventory and payments need your existing backend integration.
